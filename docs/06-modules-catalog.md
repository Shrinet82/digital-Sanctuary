# 6 · Modules Catalog

The full therapeutic toolkit — 30+ modules grouped by what someone's going through. For each: **what it does** and the honest **"Helps you:"** line. Tags note the framework and whether it's rule-based or uses the narrow AI allowlist.

Legend: 🧮 = rule-based · ✨ = optional narrow AI · framework in *italics*.

---

## 🌊 Immediate relief (the Headspace/Calm layer)

Short, gentle, leave-anytime tools for the "I'm not okay this second" moments.

- **Ground & Settle** — 60–180s paced-breathing flow with a breathing orb and before/after rating; longer exhale calms the nervous system. **Helps you:** take the physical edge off panic in under two minutes, anywhere. 🧮 *NHS · CCI*
- **5-4-3-2-1 Grounding** — guided sensory countdown. **Helps you:** interrupt a spiral by anchoring to your senses. 🧮 *Grounding*
- **Audio Practices** — body-scan, progressive muscle relaxation, calm-focus tracks. **Helps you:** wind down for sleep or reset mid-day. 🧮 *Relaxation*
- **TIPP** — DBT crisis skill: Temperature, Intense exercise, Paced breathing, Paired muscle relaxation. **Helps you:** bring down an emotional storm fast enough to think again. 🧮 *DBT*

## 🌀 Anxiety & worry

- **Worry Sorter** — decision tree sorting a worry into actionable / uncertain / needs-real-help, then routing it. **Helps you:** stop treating every worry the same way. 🧮 *CBT · CCI*
- **Thought Record (ABC)** — 5-step CBT diary (situation → body → thought → wider view → re-rate). **Helps you:** see a thought isn't a fact, and watch belief soften. ✨recap *CBT*
- **Behavioural Experiment** — test a feared prediction safely; built-in safety gate. **Helps you:** gather real evidence the scary prediction usually doesn't come true. 🧮 *CBT*
- **Worry Window** — park an uncertain worry to a scheduled time, with a refocus card meanwhile. **Helps you:** stop worries hijacking your whole day. 🧮 *CBT*
- **Problem-Solving Ladder** — define issue, list options, pick smallest step, schedule review. **Helps you:** turn vague dread into one doable move. 🧮 *CBT*
- **Maintenance Map** — early warning signs, supports, next steps. **Helps you:** catch a downturn early. 🧮 *Relapse prevention*

## 🌻 Low mood & depression

- **One Small Action** — one achievable/pleasant/connecting action; outcomes done/partly/moved/not today. **Helps you:** break the low-energy loop with one tiny win. ✨shrink *Behavioural activation · WHO*
- **Energy-Aware Week** — capacity-first weekly planner; move or drop anything, no penalty. **Helps you:** rebuild gentle rhythm without a rigid schedule. 🧮 *Behavioural activation*
- **Values to Action** — pick a value → one tiny voluntary 2-minute step. **Helps you:** act from what matters even when motivation is gone. 🧮 *ACT · WHO*
- **Before/After Activity Check** — rate mood/effort/pleasure/achievement around an action. **Helps you:** notice doing things lifts you slightly. 🧮 *Behavioural activation*
- **Setback & Staying-Well Plan** — warning signs, supports, barriers, help routes. **Helps you:** have a plan ready before a low hits. 🧮 *Relapse prevention*
- **Opposite Action** — DBT skill: a small move against an unhelpful emotional pull. **Helps you:** gently act against the urge to hide. 🧮 *DBT*

## ⚡ ADHD & executive function

Neurodiversity-affirming — describing friction and support needs, never defects or laziness.

- **What's Blocking Me?** — name the friction (initiation, prioritising, time blindness, overwhelm, distraction, working memory, emotional friction) and get routed. **Helps you:** treat the actual blocker instead of "being lazy". 🧮 *Focus Forward*
- **Task Decomposer** — one goal → observable micro-steps with a <2-minute first step. **Helps you:** beat task-paralysis by making the first move impossibly small. ✨wording *Focus Forward*
- **Priority Lens** — sort tasks Now/Schedule/Shrink/Let-go → 1-2-3 list capped at three. **Helps you:** decide what actually goes first. 🧮 *Focus Forward*
- **Time Container** — visual focus timer with start ritual, big countdown, finish ritual + note-to-future-you, cost-free "restart gently". **Helps you:** make time visible and lower the cost of (re)starting. 🧮 *Focus Forward*
- **Focus Plan** — configure distractor reduction, body-doubling, location, sensory support, reminders. **Helps you:** set up your environment so focus is possible. 🧮 *Focus Forward*
- **Weekly Reset** — review what worked, reschedule without penalty, protect capacity. **Helps you:** start each week guilt-free. 🧮 *Focus Forward*
- **Body-Doubling Room** — opt-in, privacy-reviewed virtual co-working. **Helps you:** borrow accountability to get moving. 🧮 *Later phase*

## 🧭 Substance use · harm reduction

Non-stigmatising; reduction / safer use / abstinence / learning are all valid. Lives in the extra-private vault.

- **Immediate Safety Gateway** — always-available route to emergency response, overdose/naloxone info, local directories. **Helps you:** reach verified help instantly, no chatbot in the way. 🧮 *SAMHSA*
- **My Goal, My Direction** — you choose your goal; no moralised ranking. **Helps you:** engage without being judged. 🧮 *Motivational · harm reduction*
- **Use Pattern & Context Log** — optional private logging of context/craving/mood/supports (never procurement details). **Helps you:** make triggers visible. 🧮 *CHARM*
- **Trigger & Alternative Map** — map triggers, pre-choose an alternative, set supports. **Helps you:** shorten the gap between urge and reaction. 🧮 *ATTC TRUST*
- **Mooring Lines** — weekly protective behaviours (sleep, meals, movement, contact, appointments); counts days, never scores. **Helps you:** see and build on the anchors already in your life. 🧮 *ATTC TRUST*
- **Lapse Learning Review** — non-punitive review: context, warning signs, what helped, one adjustment, support option. **Helps you:** treat a lapse as information, not failure. 🧮 *NICRO · CHARM*

## 📈 Tracking, insights & the glue

- **15-Second Check-In** — optional sliders (distress/energy/attention/urge) + "what would help most". **Helps you:** get a personalised next step in seconds. 🧮
- **Smart Recommendation** — transparent rule engine → one primary action + up to two alternatives, always showing *why*. **Helps you:** avoid decision paralysis. 🧮
- **Mood & Factor Tracking** — Bearable-style mood + custom factors over time. **Helps you:** spot what genuinely moves your mood. 🧮
- **What Helps Me Most** — private cross-module view of top-rated tools, organised by need not diagnosis. **Helps you:** learn your own toolkit. 🧮
- **Streak-Free Trends** — soft rolling weekly charts, zero streaks/scores/pass-fail. **Helps you:** see progress without a chain to guiltily break. 🧮
- **Export & Delete** — download everything or erase your account in one tap. **Helps you:** stay in full control of your data. 🧮

### The connective layer (why one app, not four)

Every module writes to the same private history, and one check-in feeds every recommendation — which is why we can show a **"what helps me most across everything"** view. A meditation app can't see your ADHD tasks; a task app can't see your mood. **We can.** That's the whole reason to build one app.

# 1 · Product Blueprint

## The one-sentence pitch

A single, low-friction web app that meets you wherever you are today — panic, paralysis, flat mood, or a craving — surfaces the **one smallest helpful next step**, and quietly tracks your patterns so you can see yourself getting steadier over time.

> **In plain terms:** a friendly dashboard that asks "how are you right now?" and hands you exactly one useful thing to do — while keeping a private diary of what worked, so it gets smarter about helping you.

## Who it's for

People living with one or more of: **anxiety, depression/low mood, ADHD (executive dysfunction), and substance use.** These conditions overlap constantly, yet the app market treats them separately. Our users currently pay for and juggle several single-purpose apps.

## Why now / why us

The competitors are excellent at **one thing each**. Our edge is the **connective layer**: one check-in feeds every module, and a private "what helps me most across everything" view that no single-purpose app can build — because a meditation app can't see your ADHD tasks and a task app can't see your mood. Add a safety-first, streak-free, shame-free design the wellness market mostly ignores, and that's the gap.

## The feature map — one app, several rivals replaced

| What the user needs | Our feature | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| Calm down / de-stress | Ground & Settle, audio practices, grounding flows | Headspace · Calm |
| Break down an overwhelming task | Task Decomposer (goal → 2-minute first step) | Goblin Tools |
| Structure the day / focus | Time Container, Energy-Aware Week, Priority Lens | Tiimo |
| Track mood & what affects it | 15-second check-ins, factor tracking, trend views | Moodfit · Bearable |
| Work through anxious/depressed thinking | Thought Record, Worry Sorter, Behavioural Activation, Values to Action | self-help PDFs |
| Manage substance use safely | Trigger Map, Mooring Lines, Lapse Review, Safety Gateway | scattered worksheets |
| Stay safe in a crisis | Always-on urgent help + deterministic routing | (most apps skip this) |

See [docs/07-competitor-comparison.md](./07-competitor-comparison.md) for the full scored table.

## What success looks like (early)

- A user can arrive in distress and reach a helpful action in **under 30 seconds**.
- A returning user finds their history intact and a **gentle, honest** trend view they *want* to look at.
- Nothing in the product produces shame, and every crisis path has been tested in each supported region.
